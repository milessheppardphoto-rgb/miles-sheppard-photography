About

Pictures that look the way the meal feels.

I'm Miles Sheppard, a Chicago photographer focused on food and drink. I work with natural light and real tables, so a plate looks the way it does when it reaches a guest.

I shoot menus, new openings, cocktail programs and brand content for restaurants and bars. Every project starts with a short conversation about what the pictures need to do, and I plan the shoot around that.

If you're in Chicago and need photos for a menu, a website or social media, I'd like to hear about it.
